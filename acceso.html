      Source lineage and methodology

      From documented source families to the authenticated dashboard

      01Data sources

      Contextual indicators publish source names and dataset identifiers in the inventory above. The separate immigration-flow view does not currently expose row-level source attribution.

      02Processing

      The project documentation describes pipeline steps for cleaning, validation, duplicate handling and missing-value treatment. This dashboard does not expose transformation logs, so it cannot verify which rule changed an individual record.

      03Country & City datasets

      Country and City observations and forecast rows are read from the published observed_latest and forecasts_latest views in the migrai schema.

      04Model evaluation

      Production forecasts remain separate from backtesting. The dashboard reads published observed/predicted pairs and horizon/entity metrics from protected backtest views; these are diagnostics from the model-selection backtest, not an independent holdout.

      05Production forecasts

      Forecast status, target year, horizon, model label and interval bounds are displayed only when the corresponding fields are present and valid in the published rows.

      06Supabase → Dashboard

      Supabase Auth gates access. The static GitHub Pages client makes authenticated, read-only queries to the approved views, including the published context and backtest views. No secret or service-role key is used by the browser.

      A published year range describes rows currently available, not the complete set of years expected by the pipeline. The current views do not expose an expected entity-year manifest.

      Transparency and scope

      What the production views can describe—and what they cannot establish

      01
      Different measures

      Country predicts annual immigration flows in persons per year. City predicts foreign-resident share as a percentage of city residents; these measures are not comparable.

      02
      No causal or individual inference

      Aggregate patterns do not identify why a person moves, individual behaviour, undocumented flows, policy choices or specific service needs.

      03
      Uncertainty bands

      Where published, intervals represent modelled uncertainty under the model assumptions. They are not guarantees or probability claims about any individual outcome.

      04
      Five-year horizon

      Longer horizons compound uncertainty and depend on assumptions about continuation of observed patterns. City views currently expose only the horizons present in their records.

      05
      Updates

      The dashboard reads the latest published views. Refresh timing follows the upstream pipeline; no fixed schedule is claimed unless run timestamps are supplied.

      06
      Production vs. experiments

      Only values in the production views appear here. Experimental outputs and backtest metrics are not substituted for released forecasts.

      ABOUT THIS VIEW

      Observed data and forecasts

      Country figures represent annual immigration flows (persons per year), while City figures represent the foreign-resident share of a city's population (percent of residents); these measures are not directly comparable. Solid marks show observed values; hollow marks show forecasts.

      Map colours and rankings use only values available for the selected year. Missing values are not treated as zero. The European total does not display an aggregated uncertainty band because no validated method for combining country bands is available here.
